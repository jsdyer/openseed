What “available” means

A scheduled check fetched and parsed the small torrent file, or requested the first byte of the listed research file. It did not download the full payload, verify every file’s contents or measure swarm seeders. A link that responded then may fail now. Failed checks remain visible.

Linux installers contain packages under individual licences. Archive licences are supplied by the uploader; verify rights for your intended use. Only explicitly allowlisted open licences enter the archive and research collections. Checksums are source-published metadata, not a completed verification of a file on your computer.

For Zenodo records with several files, the card links to the smallest listed file and shows the record’s total separately. Open the source record to choose other files.
